### Favorite Series: Hunter x Hunter (Chimera Ant Arc)

The **Chimera Ant Arc** is widely considered the peak of *Hunter x Hunter* because of how it flips the moral roles of its characters—turning bloodthirsty monsters into deeply empathetic beings while pushing human heroes to their darkest extremes.

### The Moral Inversion
**Monsters Learning Humanity (Meruem):** Born as a ruthless King who views humans solely as food, Meruem slowly discovers empathy, respect, and love through his Gungi matches with **Komugi**, a blind human girl. By the end, he chooses to spend his final moments playing a board game rather than conquering the world.
**Humans Showing Monstrosity (Gon & Netero):** Consumed by grief and rage over Kite, **Gon** abandons his innocence and sacrifices everything to brutally destroy Neferpitou. Meanwhile, **Chairman Netero** weaponizes humanity's capacity for malice, using the *Poor Man's Rose*—a cheap, mass-produced biological bomb—to defeat the Ant King.

> *"Human potential for evolution is limitless... or should I say, human potential for malice."*

| Character | Species | Arc Trajectory |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **Gon Freecss** | Human | Descends from a bright-eyed kid into vengeful darkness |
| **Isaac Netero** | Human | Relies on humanity's bottomless malice to win at any cost |
